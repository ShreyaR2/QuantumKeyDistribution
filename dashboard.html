Protocol Info

Alice prepares random qubits in two bases: rectilinear + and diagonal ×.

Bob measures each qubit in a randomly chosen basis. Matching bases yield the sifted key.

QBER ≥ 11% signals eavesdropping. Eve's intercept-resend attack introduces ~25% errors.
