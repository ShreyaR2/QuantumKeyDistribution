Laser pulses & decoy check
Alice mixes bright (signal), dim (decoy) and empty (vacuum) pulses. On an honest fibre each click rate matches its prediction (ratio ≈ 1×). Eve can't tell the pulses apart, so her attack breaks the pattern.
